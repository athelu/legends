# Nephilim
Nephilim are daemons from the elemental planes that manifest on the prime material as giant humanoids. Their physical form is elemental energy made flesh — a Fire Nephilim bleeds magma, a Stone Nephilim sheds gravel when struck, an Ice Nephilim leaves frost wherever it steps, and an Air Nephilim's footsteps sound like wind through empty corridors. They are not elementals wearing humanoid shape. They are something older and stranger — elemental energy that chose a form with hands, with eyes, with something approaching a face.
They are territorial and tend toward solitary existence. Most have human-level intelligence or better, but their priorities are alien to mortal sensibilities. A Nephilim will keep its word with absolute literalness and is deeply offended by what it perceives as disrespect. Summoners who address them as tools will find cooperation difficult. Summoners who acknowledge what they are will find them considerably more tractable.
Nephilim do not exist at TR 1/8 or 1/4. The smallest Nephilim specimen is Large. Anything less is not a Nephilim — it is an elemental.
Creature Type: Nephilim (Giant)
All Nephilim share: Large or Huge size. Powerful Build — counts as one size category larger for carrying, pushing, dragging, and lifting. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep in the conventional sense; they sustain themselves on elemental energy from their home plane.
Elemental immunities and resistances by subtype are noted in individual entries.
# Fae
Fae are creatures of the elemental planes whose courts have spread across those realms over millennia. They are not defined by a single element — a Fae may be found in the courts of Fire as readily as in the courts of Water, and the same creature may have allegiances that span multiple planes simultaneously. What defines them is not their element but their nature: mischievous, capricious, bound by ancient rules of obligation and exchange that mortals rarely understand completely and almost never understand in time.
Fae honor the letter of agreements with absolute precision and the spirit of them with considerable flexibility. A summoner who gives a Fae imprecise instructions will receive precise execution of something other than what they intended. This is not malice — it is the Fae's fundamental relationship with language, which they treat as a medium for exact meaning rather than approximate communication. A Fae told to "deal with" an enemy has been given genuine latitude to interpret "deal with" however seems most interesting.
Low-tier Fae are mischievous and small, their power more disruptive than destructive. Mid-tier Fae are capable combatants and considerably more alien in their reasoning. High-tier Fae are beings of genuine power whose cooperation represents a significant advantage and whose displeasure tends to persist across multiple encounters, multiple years, and occasionally multiple generations.
Pure Fae have no inherent moral orientation. Corrupted Fae — those that have spent extended time near Negative energy ley lines — are a different matter and are noted where relevant.
Creature Type: Fey
All Fae share: Fortune on saving throws against charm effects. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep in the conventional sense — they sustain themselves on elemental energy and the ambient magic of whatever plane they currently inhabit.

Humanoid
Many of the challenges that the characters will face will be the denizens of their world. These are normal humans or elves, who for whatever reason have become an obstacle. Combat should not be the only outcome in any encounter, but tactics have been provided for most of these threats.
Feel free to replace the weapons that these characters use for any that make sense for the given scenario. Some entries will have Variants; a quick note on how to differentiate these threats or create a leader. 

Beasts
Beasts are native fauna of the world of Athelu. Some are mundane while others border on the realm of the extraordinary. These creatures occur naturally, although some might have been encouraged through selective breeding.
None of these entries should require a magical or mystical reason for occuring within the world. 

Air Daemons
Elementals represent Daemons from the four elemental planes. This includes creatures composed of the raw material of these planes as well as the alien creatures that make there homes in these planes. 
This section will be broken into each of the elemental planes and then by the denizen type.

Air Elementals
Air Elementals are not creatures that move through wind. They are wind given will. Where Fire Elementals express themselves through heat and consumption, Air Elementals express themselves through movement, pressure, and the denial of stillness. They do not hold ground — they make ground untenable for others.
They are the fastest of the four elemental categories and the most difficult to physically restrain. Grappling an Air Elemental is an exercise in trying to hold a storm in both hands. Their intelligence scales faster than other elementals as they increase in power — even mid-tier Air Elementals communicate clearly and understand nuanced instructions.
They will not calm wind they are producing, remain still when their nature demands movement, or act against currents they have already set in motion. A summoner who needs something pinned in place should call an Earth Elemental. A summoner who needs something scattered, struck from a distance, or pursued across open ground calls Air.
Creature Type: Elemental (Air)
All Air Elementals share: Immune to lightning damage, poison damage, Poisoned condition, disease, exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Unconscious. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.

Air Nephilim
Air Nephilim manifest where the Plane of Air intersects the prime material most strongly — high mountain peaks, exposed cliff faces, the upper reaches of storm systems. Their flesh is dense air made solid, their eyes the pale grey of high-altitude sky. They move with a fluid economy that makes their size deceptive — an Air Nephilim covering ground is faster than it looks, and they carry the wind with them.
They are the most communicative of the four Nephilim subtypes. Even lower-tier Air Nephilim express opinions about their instructions and will point out what they consider to be tactical errors. Whether the summoner finds this useful or irritating depends on the summoner.
Air Nephilim type traits: Immune to lightning damage. Resistance to cold damage (+2 DR). Elemental Aura (wind, passive) — intensity scales with TR, noted per entry.

Fire Daemons

Fire Elementals
Elementals are not creatures that use fire. They are fire given will. The distinction matters when a summoner gives instructions — a Fire Elemental ordered to "guard this room" will express that order through heat, light, and the threat of burning. An Elemental ordered to "stop that person" will interpret stopping as combustion unless told otherwise.
Fire Elementals sustain themselves on the energy of the Plane of Fire. On the prime material, they feed on ambient heat and combustible material. A Fire Elemental denied fuel grows restless; one given abundant fuel grows stronger than its base state. Summoners who call Fire Elementals near kindling should account for this.
They will not smother flames, protect something flammable from burning, or act against the spread of fire they have been part of creating. These are not failures of compliance — they are categorical impossibilities.
Creature Type: Elemental (Fire)
All Fire Elementals share: Immune to fire damage, poison damage, Poisoned condition, disease, exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Unconscious. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.

Fire Nephilim
Fire Nephilim are born of volcanic regions where the Plane of Fire intersects the prime material most strongly — deep calderas, active lava fields, places where the ground itself is barely solid. They bleed magma when wounded. Their skin is perpetually warm to the touch even at lower tiers; at higher tiers, proximity becomes dangerous without invitation.
They are the most aggressive of the four Nephilim subtypes, sharing the Fire Elemental's fundamental inability to back away from something combustible. Unlike Fire Elementals, they understand consequences — they simply often judge that the consequences are worth it.
Fire Nephilim type traits: Immune to fire damage. Resistance to lightning damage (+2 DR). Elemental Aura (fire, passive) — intensity scales with TR, noted per entry.



Earth Daemons

Earth Elementals
Earth Elementals do not move through stone. They are stone given will. They are the slowest of the four elemental categories and the most difficult to damage, destroy, or move. Where Air Elementals deny ground by making it dangerous to occupy, Earth Elementals deny ground by occupying it themselves and refusing to leave.
Their intelligence develops more slowly than other elementals as power increases — even high-tier Earth Elementals communicate in simple, declarative terms and respond poorly to instructions that require nuance or conditional logic. What they understand perfectly is a position, a target, and a line they are not to let anything cross. Give them those three things and they will perform without error until something geologically significant stops them.
They will not leave ground they have been assigned to hold, break stone they have been ordered to preserve, or abandon a Grappled target once they have committed to holding it. A summoner who needs flexibility calls Air. A summoner who needs something to stay exactly where it is until further notice calls Earth.
Creature Type: Elemental (Earth)
All Earth Elementals share: Immune to non-magical piercing and slashing damage, poison damage, Poisoned condition, disease, exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Unconscious. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.

Earth Nephilim
Earth Nephilim manifest where the Plane of Earth intersects the prime material most heavily — deep mountain roots, ancient geological formations, cavern systems so old they predate most surface civilizations. They are the densest of the four Nephilim subtypes in every sense. Their flesh is granite and basalt. They shed gravel when struck hard enough. They move with the deliberate economy of something that has never needed to hurry because nothing has ever successfully run from them.
They are the least communicative of the four subtypes. Earth Nephilim speak when they have something to say and not otherwise. Their instructions are understood precisely and executed without elaboration or deviation. A summoner who gives clear, simple instructions to a Earth Nephilim will find them followed with a thoroughness that borders on geological.
Earth Nephilim type traits: Immune to non-magical piercing and slashing damage. Resistance to non-magical bludgeoning damage (+2 DR). Elemental Aura (earth tremor, passive) — intensity scales with TR, noted per entry.


Water Daemons
Water Elementals
Water Elementals are not creatures that move through water. They are water given will. They are patient, adaptive, and relentless in the way that erosion is relentless — not through force alone but through persistence and the exploitation of whatever gap presents itself. Where Earth Elementals hold ground and Fire Elementals consume it, Water Elementals reshape it, wearing down opposition the way a river wears down stone.
They are significantly more dangerous near water than away from it. A Water Elemental on dry ground is capable and threatening. The same creature beside a lake or river is something else entirely — faster, harder to hit, able to drag enemies into an environment where the Elemental has every advantage and the enemy has none. Summoners who can choose their battlefield will always choose water when calling these creatures.
They will not seal gaps water is already flowing through, abandon a creature they are actively engulfing, or leave a body of water they have been assigned to guard. A Water Elemental given a pond to protect will be in that pond when the summoner returns. Whether anything else is still alive nearby depends on what approached.
Creature Type: Elemental (Water)
All Water Elementals share: Immune to cold damage, poison damage, Poisoned condition, disease, exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Unconscious. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.


Water Nephilim
Ice Nephilim manifest where the Plane of Water, in its coldest and most concentrated form, intersects the prime material — deep glaciers, polar ice fields, mountain peaks above the snowline where the cold is a physical presence rather than an absence of heat. They are the Water Resonance Summoner's heavy combat option, built from ice rather than liquid, and they express their elemental nature through cold, weight, and the kind of stillness that precedes a glacier's advance.
They are patient in a way that distinguishes them from other Nephilim subtypes. Ice Nephilim do not rush. They build pressure steadily — stacking cold conditions, slowing movement, grinding down opposition the way a glacier grinds down bedrock. A summoner who wants fast, decisive results should call a Fire Nephilim. A summoner who wants the enemy to be unable to effectively fight back by the third round of combat should call Ice.
Ice Nephilim type traits: Immune to cold damage. Resistance to non-magical piercing and slashing damage (+2 DR). Elemental Aura (cold, passive) — intensity scales with TR, noted per entry.


Outsiders
Aetherim (Positive)
Aetherim are beings of the Positive plane — life energy, vitality, and the sustaining force that underlies all living things given will and form. They are not gods, not servants of any single deity of the Eight, and not angels in the sense of divine messengers. They are what the Positive plane produces when it generates entities capable of acting in the material world: beings of concentrated life energy whose fundamental purpose is the preservation and reinforcement of living things.
They do not lie. This is not a moral position — it is a categorical feature of their nature. Aetherim perceive the truth of things directly and cannot produce deliberate falsehood any more than fire can produce cold. A summoner who asks an Aetherim a direct question will receive a direct answer, including answers the summoner would prefer not to hear.
They will not harm living creatures except in defense of other living creatures. They will not reinforce undead, fiends, or creatures whose fundamental nature is the consumption or destruction of life energy. They will not assist in actions whose primary purpose is the ending of life rather than its protection. A summoner who needs something killed should call something else. A summoner who needs something kept alive, protected, healed, or revealed will find Aetherim singularly effective.
The Positive plane's relationship with the Pantheon of Eight is close but not subordinate. Aetherim acknowledge the gods of the Eight as significant powers whose interests frequently align with Positive plane values. They do not take orders from priests on the gods' behalf — they make their own assessments.
Creature Type: Aetherim
All Aetherim share: Immune to poison damage, Poisoned condition, disease, fear. Fortune on saving throws against magic. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.

Fiends (Negative)
Fiends are beings of the Negative plane's lower reaches — not the entropy and decay that flow through the plane as natural forces, but the will and appetite that those forces generate when they coalesce into something that wants. They are not evil in the way that a disease is evil, without intent or preference. They want things. They want to consume, to dominate, to cause suffering, to acquire power. The distinction between fiend categories is largely a matter of what they want most and how sophisticated their methods for getting it are.
A summoner who calls Fiends is negotiating with something that is calculating the exchange from the moment the connection opens. Lower-tier Fiends are calculating poorly — they are impulsive, short-sighted, and their assessment of what they can gain from the arrangement is usually wrong. Higher-tier Fiends are calculating very well indeed, and their assessment of what they can gain is usually right. A summoner who calls a high-tier Fiend and expects it to behave like a tool has made a significant error in their model of what they are dealing with.
Fiends honor agreements that benefit them. They do not honor agreements that do not. The distinction between these categories is something they determine unilaterally and with considerable creativity. A summoner who binds a Fiend with precise, comprehensive terms will find it cooperative. A summoner who leaves gaps will find those gaps exploited with ingenuity that is, in its own way, impressive.
Fiends will not protect living creatures from harm except as a means to some other end. They will not heal. They will not perform actions that meaningfully benefit others at cost to themselves unless those actions serve a longer strategy. They are, without exception, working toward something.
Creature Type: Fiend
All Fiends share: Immune to poison damage, Poisoned condition. Resistance to fire, cold, lightning damage (+2 DR each). Fortune on saving throws against magic. Do not need air, food, drink, or sleep.

Aberrations
The Space between planes is not empty. It is not darkness, not void, not absence. It is something that has no useful analogy in the experience of any creature that evolved on a material plane, because material plane existence is defined by location — by the fact that things occupy specific positions relative to other things, that distance is consistent, that cause precedes effect in a reliable sequence. The Space between planes does not operate on these principles. Things exist there without occupying positions. Distance does not accumulate. Effects occur without proximate causes. What lives there has adapted to these conditions over spans of time that make the age of any material world seem brief.
Aberrations are not alien in the way that a creature from a distant land is alien — unfamiliar customs, different language, values that can eventually be translated into terms you recognize. They are alien in the way that a color outside the visible spectrum is alien to someone who has never experienced it. Their cognition does not map onto mortal frameworks. Their goals are not hostile so much as orthogonal. When they damage things, it is frequently because they are doing something else and the damage is incidental. When they appear to be communicating, they may be — or they may be doing something entirely different that the receiving mind is interpreting as communication because that is the only framework it has available.
They do not require air, food, drink, or sleep. They do not have biological drives in any recognizable sense. They have imperatives that are sometimes describable as goals, but the goals rarely translate cleanly into language designed by creatures with biological needs and social structures.
A summoner who calls an Aberration is calling something that is following its own imperatives while choosing, for reasons that may or may not be comprehensible, to also follow the summoner's instructions for the duration of the binding.
Creature Type: Aberration
All Aberrations share: Darkvision 120 ft. Immunity to the Frightened condition. Immunity to Charm effects. Does not need air, food, drink, or sleep. Takes full damage from all energy types — they have no resistances native to their type, because they did not evolve in an environment where these categories were meaningful.
Individual entries note additional capabilities. Aberrations are the only daemon category without a universal vulnerability — they are difficult to counter with a single energy type.

Shadow Creatures
The Shadow Plane is not darkness. It is the absence of everything that light implies — warmth, growth, the forward movement of living things. It is a plane where color has faded to grey, where sound travels wrong, where distances do not behave consistently, and where the things that live there have adapted to scarcity by becoming very good at taking what little exists from whatever has more of it.
Shadow creatures are not undead. They are living things shaped by a lightless environment where life energy is sparse and every creature competes for it. Most are predators by necessity rather than malice. They hunt because the Shadow Plane does not offer alternatives. When summoned to the prime material — a plane of abundant light, warmth, and life energy — they find themselves in what amounts to extreme surplus. Their behavior in this environment reflects that: they are acutely aware of the richness around them and drawn to it in ways that require active management from a summoner who wants them focused on a specific target rather than the nearest warm body.
They are not inherently evil. They are shaped by a plane that rewards predation and punishes hesitation. The distinction matters primarily to a summoner who is deciding how much latitude to give them.

Positive energy is their universal weakness. It is not merely damaging — it is antithetical to what they are in a way that fire is antithetical to ice. A Shadow creature struck by significant Positive energy does not simply take damage. It experiences the contact as a fundamental wrongness.
